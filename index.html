The method

Five stages. One new era.

A tarot-inspired process combining creative self-inquiry, persona design, practical behavioral changes, guided suggestion, and a deliberate public debut.

I

The Fool — Remember

Explore childhood memories, early fascinations, natural inclinations, and the creative impulses that existed before outside expectations shaped your expression.

Outcome: Your original creative profile
II

The Persona — Create

Use your discoveries to define the next artistic identity: its character, archetypes, values, visual language, and way of moving through the world. Name it if you choose; change that name as you evolve.

Outcome: Your Rockstar Persona Blueprint
III

The Programming — Practice

Choose three high-leverage changes: one behavior to stop, one to start, and one environment to change. Begin practicing your persona in the choices you make every day.

Outcome: Your three-change embodiment protocol
IV

The Manifesto — Reinforce

Collaboratively create an in-depth, personalized guided affirmation and visualization during your first session. Receive the recording to revisit each morning as a tool for reflection and mental rehearsal.

Outcome: Your personal audio manifesto
V

The Liberation — Debut

In week three, plan and execute a meaningful, high-visibility introduction of your new persona: a performance, visual reveal, creative statement, or other bold artistic act aligned with your goals.

Outcome: Your liberation initiation and integration

Questions

Before you begin.

Do I have to be a musician?

No. This is for established artists across music, visual art, film, fashion, performance, and other creative disciplines. “Rockstar” describes a bold, deliberate creative identity—not a genre.

Do I need to know my new persona already?

No. You can begin with a feeling, a creative ambition, or a sense that your current expression no longer fits. The application and private process help you clarify it.

What if my persona changes?

That's expected. Your chosen persona name is optional and can evolve. The work is about intentional creative expression, not locking you into a permanent label.

Is this hypnotherapy?

The experience incorporates principles from hypnotherapy through personalized affirmations, guided visualization, and creative mental rehearsal. It is not clinical hypnotherapy, psychotherapy, or treatment for a mental health condition. If you have a mental health condition, seek care from a qualified healthcare professional.

What happens after I apply?

You'll receive a personalized reading and a next-step recommendation. Applicants who appear suited to private mentorship may be invited to book a discovery call. Other applicants may be offered a self-guided option.

Is enrollment guaranteed?

No. Private mentorship is limited to artists whose goals and readiness align with the experience.
